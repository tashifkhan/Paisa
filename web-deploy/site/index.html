Billi in three minutes

A walk through the app, from the first screen to backups.

One text in, one row out

Parsers for around 140 Indian banks, from the PennyWise project.

  • No double counting. A rescan skips texts it already read.
  • Categories that learn. Fix a merchant once.
  • One payment, one row. UPI alert and bank SMS merge.

See where the month went

Charts by date range, monthly caps per category, and debts in one place.

What leaves your phone

Bank SMS and alerts
Nothing. Billi reads them on the device and they stay there.
Your transactions
A database on the phone. There is no Billi account or server.
Backups
Only to Google Drive, Nextcloud or a file you pick. Add a password and the file is AES‑256‑GCM encrypted.
AI
Only with a key you add. Or Gemini Nano, which runs on supported phones.
